Skyfall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Skyfall is a single-player game made by Daniel Burgin. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

What we collect

Nothing. Skyfall has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (feathers, wingsuits, upgrades, records, awards, your pilot name and your settings) is saved in a file on your device. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also wipe your progress in Settings → Reset progress.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Skyfall may send your best scores and earned awards to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards and in your achievements. Apple handles this under Apple's privacy policy. We don't receive or store it.

The game engine

Skyfall is built with Unity. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information about your device (such as its model and operating system version) to help Unity improve its engine. It is not linked to you. See Unity's privacy policy.

Children

Skyfall doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: danielburgin98@gmail.com.
